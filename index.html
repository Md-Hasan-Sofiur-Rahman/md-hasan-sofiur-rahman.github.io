About Me

I am a Scientific Officer at the Bangladesh Institute of Nuclear Agriculture (BINA) and a PhD Research Scholar at Western Sydney University. My research focuses on stress biology, plant breeding, photosynthesis, photoprotection, and functional genomics for improving crop adaptation and productivity under changing environmental conditions.
